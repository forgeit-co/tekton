# Tekton product design

Tekton's visual identity is not yet formally selected. Do not introduce a logo, brand palette, or visual system as an implied operator decision; the product owner should make and record those choices when needed.

## Experience principles

- Optimize for the operator's clarity and effort, not for the smallest amount of software.
- Each screen should answer: where am I, what is blocking me, and what should I do next?
- Show sources and verification dates with claims; distinguish known facts from items still to verify.
- Never rely on color alone to communicate state. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, accessible labels, and clear loading, empty, error, and disconnected states.
- Keep the interface local-first, calm, and explicit about what an agent can and cannot do.

These principles follow the [functional specification](docs/functional-spec.md) §§1 and 13. The palette, typography, iconography, and logo remain open for a deliberate design decision.
